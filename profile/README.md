**Here I am. What can we make possible?**

We are building a global community around practical accessibility. People with disabilities, craftspeople, makers, developers, designers, and AI practitioners work toward goals defined by the people who will use the solutions.

We want to help people find, adapt, reproduce, and maintain tools that work in everyday life. We build on existing work, credit its creators, and aim to contribute improvements back.

### What we are working toward

- **Open solutions:** editable designs, code, documentation, and AI assets with clear licenses and dependencies.
- **Reproducible work:** sourcing alternatives, installation instructions, testing evidence, and maintenance guidance.
- **Useful AI:** assistance with research, adaptation, translation, and documentation, with appropriate verification.
- **People choosing their own goals:** lived experience belongs in every part of the work.

### Bring what you know

Craft, CAD, software, accessibility, AI, translation, documentation, sourcing, and independent reproduction all have a place. When a repository welcomes contributions, start with its README and contribution guide.

### Where we are now

Hineni Lab is a nonprofit initiative in development. The marketplace is an illustrative prototype; it does not currently accept real assistance requests. Repositories should state their actual maturity, licenses, and testing evidence.

[Explore the concept at hinenilab.org](https://hinenilab.org)

Open solutions do not require open personal information. Please keep private assistance requests and identifying details out of public issues.
